How It Works

Built by an
algorithm,
not a template.

Most meal apps hand you a generic plan. Wondish runs a five-stage pipeline tailored to your exact body and preferences.

Caloric Engine

Precision nutrition,
down to the calorie.

Using the Harris-Benedict equation and your activity level, we compute your exact daily energy needs — then split it across meals.

  • ✓ BMR-based calculation
  • ✓ Activity multiplier applied
  • ✓ Safe minimum floor (1200–1500 kcal)
  • ✓ Per-meal calorie budget
⚡
Safety Filters

Zero risk.
Maximum flavor.

Allergies, conditions, and personal avoidances are merged into one safety layer. Nothing slips through.

  • ✓ Allergy-derived ingredient bans
  • ✓ Medical condition restrictions
  • ✓ Preference exclusions
  • ✓ One merged safety set
